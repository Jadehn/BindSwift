Does Bindswift cost extra?

Quoting is free. If you decide to buy, there's a flat $50 Bindswift service fee, charged right before your policy is bound. You'll see it on your quote and sign a short fee agreement first. If no policy is bound, you aren't charged. Bindswift also earns the standard commission insurers pay brokers.

Who is my broker?

Bindswift is built by Howard Analytics, a licensed New York property & casualty agency. A real broker reviews every application before it goes to a carrier.

What if my landlord or GC needs to be listed on the certificate?

Tell us during the quote. We'll request additional insured status, plus primary & non-contributory and waiver of subrogation when a contract requires them.

I've been declined before. Can I still apply?

Yes. If standard carriers pass, your broker shops specialty (excess & surplus) markets built for harder-to-place risks.
